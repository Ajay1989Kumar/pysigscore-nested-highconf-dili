Unsupervised clustering of pysigscore scores — interpretation
======================================================================
Cohort: 101 drugs (94 toxic / 7 safe). Labels NOT used to fit PCA/t-SNE/k-means/hierarchical.

KEY CAVEAT — majority baseline
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Because 94/101 drugs are toxic, any clustering that puts almost everyone
in one big group gets purity/accuracy ≈ 0.931 without separating classes.
That is NOT evidence of separation. Prefer balanced accuracy, ARI, silhouette,
and PC1 AUROC.

GSVA Hallmark scores (primary pysigscore method)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Silhouette (toxic vs safe as groups): -0.025   (< 0 ⇒ classes overlap in space)
  PC1 |AUROC| (post-hoc ranking):       0.640   (modest > 0.50)
  PC1 Mann-Whitney p:                   2.26e-01
  Between/within centroid ratio:        0.526  (<1 ⇒ clouds overlap)
  k-means k=2 ARI vs labels:            -0.034   (≈ 0 ⇒ chance agreement)
  k-means post-hoc accuracy:            0.931  (≈ majority 0.931)
  k-means post-hoc balanced accuracy:   0.500  (≈ 0.5 = chance)

Across all methods (GSVA, ssGSEA, Mean, Median, Z, STACKED)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Silhouette range: negative for all feature sets
  k-means ARI: near 0 for all
  PC1 |AUROC|: ~0.59–0.64 (weak continuous axis, not discrete clusters)

VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Unsupervised structure in pysigscore Hallmark space does **not** cleanly
partition toxic vs safe drugs. Safes are **intermixed** with toxics in PCA/t-SNE
and hierarchical trees. k-means (k=2) recovers majority-toxic groups, not
a safe cluster.

The supervised nested model (GSVA top-10 + OR, AUROC ≈ 0.79) works by
**selecting a few pathways and fusing OR**, not because toxic/safe form
natural clusters in the full 50-D pathway space.

Implication: pysigscore is effective as a *featurization* for supervised
prediction / enrichment scoring, but unsupervised clustering alone is a
weak tool for “seeing” DILI class on this high-conf cohort (extreme imbalance).
